The MSc in Digital Marketing is designed to help participants understand how digital technologies can be exploited to optimise buyer awareness, sentiment and intention. You will gain in-depth knowledge and skills in PPC, SEO, Content Marketing, Social Media, e-Business Management, Web Development , Data Analysis, and develop strategic thinking know-how. You will have the opportunity to gain real-world experience by putting these skills to the test in an end-of-year practicum project which involves being paired up with a client, looking after their digital marketing for a 6 month period. DCU Business School has deep-routed connections with industry, which ensures relevant and up to date content within our curriculum.

### **John Thompson Scholarship for the MSc in Digital Marketing**

Established in 2008, the John Thompson scholarship was set up to honour the memory of John Thompson, one of Ireland's leading newspaper executives, and offers full fees for one student on the MSc in Digital Marketing. This scholarship aims to foster links between digital marketing and online journalism by encouraging graduates of business, journalism and more diverse educational backgrounds to study digital marketing integrated with an appreciation of media and online journalism. For more information and to apply see: [business.dcu.ie/john-thompson-scholarship/](https://business.dcu.ie/john-thompson-scholarship/)

## Why Do This Course?

Through a combination of lectures, workshops, and applied learning activities, the DCU MSc in Digital Marketing equips students with the strategic, analytical, and technical skills required to succeed in today's rapidly evolving digital marketplace. The programme integrates marketing theory with hands-on experience using contemporary digital tools, platforms, and approaches. Students will develop a strong foundation in core digital marketing areas including digital advertising, content marketing, web design, and digital business management. Alongside this, the programme emphasises the importance of data analytics and marketing metrics, enabling students to make informed decisions through data visualisation, statistical analysis, and performance measurement frameworks.

In the first semester, students are introduced to the fundamentals of digital marketing technologies, web design and development, research methods, and the broader organisational and global contexts in which digital marketing operates. These modules develop both practical technical capabilities and an understanding of global management challenges facing modern organisations.

The second semester builds on these foundations by focusing on strategic thinking, digital business management, and the responsible use of emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence in marketing. Students will explore how organisations design and implement digital strategies while addressing ethical considerations and leadership responsibilities in technology-enabled environments.

Finally, students apply their learning through a dissertation or applied consultancy project, allowing them to investigate a real-world marketing problem or conduct independent research under academic supervision. This experience helps students develop critical thinking, research, and problem-solving skills while preparing them for careers in digital marketing, analytics, and management.

## Programme Structure

The MSc in Digital Marketing is a 90 ECTS postgraduate programme delivered across three semesters. Students complete six core digital marketing modules alongside broader management modules before undertaking a dissertation or applied consultancy project.

| Semester 2 Modules                         | 15 ECTS   |

| Total                                      | 90 ECTS   |

## Module Insights

### Digital Marketing Mechanics &amp; Authorship

**Semester 1 + 2**

The purpose of this module is to provide the student with the technical knowledge and skills to author and communicate brand messaging using common and emerging digital marketing technologies. The module explores existing and emerging tools, concepts, frameworks, and experiences that are useful for developing in the student the capacity to evaluate, select, utilise and critique tactical brand messaging decisions in the digital domain. Students are expected to attend lectures, complete independent reading and to engage in applied learning activities on a regular basis.

**(10 ECTS)**

### Digital Advertising &amp; Communications

**Semester 1 + 2**

The module introduces students to contemporary issues in digital advertising and communications and the managing of digital advertising operations. The purpose of the module is to provide students with skills,

concepts, frameworks, and experiences that are useful for developing creative digital advertising campaigns and managing digital advertising operations, thus providing students with real-world practical experience. In addition to the operational/technical aspects of digital advertising, the module will emphasise advertising's practical, theoretical, and philosophical problems and contributions. This is essential for addressing some of the creative and ethical challenges facing organisations in the digital realm.

**(10 ECTS)**

### Data Analytics &amp; Metrics

**Semester 1 + 2**

The purpose of this module is to help develop the student's capacity to make better data-driven decisions through leveraging insights gained from data analysis and to motivate students' appreciation of current needs for marketing metrics and to develop a comprehensive understanding of strategy-based performance measurement frameworks. The module aims to equip students with a variety of data visualization techniques and the knowledge of a variety of tools and statistical techniques to make sense of the emergence and exponential growth of big data. The content of this module is delivered mainly through lecturers, case studies and in class demonstrations.

**(10 ECTS)**

### Applied Web Design &amp; Development

**Semester 1**

This module will introduce students to the architecture and workings of the world wide web, and to the principles of designing a usable website. It will address the technical structure of the WWW and practical aspects of web authoring as well as the design of websites. It will also introduce the tools and techniques for constructing, optimising and maintaining websites. It will address design issue including requirements specification, user experience and design principles, and usability guidelines for the development and deployment of marketing and e-commerce websites for the global consumer.

**(5 ECTS)**

### Digital Transformation &amp; Sustainable Change

**Semester 1**

This module provides a conceptual foundation in the electronic marketplace and its technological, business, and societal implications.

It will discuss key e-commerce constructs, design e-business models, and examine strategies for digital transformation and explore risk management, ethical considerations, and security challenges in the rapidly evolving digital landscape.

Focus is on developing sustainable digital strategies, understanding online consumer behavior, and leveraging emerging technologies.

**(5 ECTS)**

### Global Management Challenges

**Semester 1**

This innovative module is aimed at equipping students to work effectively in a global marketplace and to enrich their learning experience with diversity of perspective and real-world application. The module examines the international business environment and the integration and disruption of global markets. An analysis of the economic, political, and legal factors that shape the age of globalisation come under consideration, as does the relevant data that international managers can utilise to inform better decision making.

In addition, transversal skills related to effective collaboration, cross-cultural communication and global sensitivity will also be a focus of this module, where teams comprised of diverse international backgrounds will be required to work together to solve an industry-related challenge related to sustainability.

As businesses increasingly span geographical regions and time-zones, particularly those of developing economies, a focus of the module is on how managers respond to new challenges within the business and societal context and ensure greater sustainability of operations and resources. In accordance, the module pushes students to work together to generate solutions aimed at building and managing strategic alliances with a focus on common goals and more sustainable business practices. Additionally, these efforts and approaches should consider corporate social responsibility and the need to protect people, stakeholders and plant while driving profit.

**(5 ECTS)**

### Research Methods

**Semester 1**

This module gives participants an understanding of the processes in undertaking research. The focus of the module will be research as part of business research in a specialist domain. The module will begin with an introduction to the research process, and will develop into an exploration of alternative approaches to research methods. Each stage of the research process will be examined: research design, ethcis, literature review, research methodologies and data gathering techniques, data analysis, drawing conclusions, making recommendations. By the end of the module participants will have a thorough grasp of the principles and practicalities of how to conduct research.

**(5 ECTS)**

### Digital Business Management

**Semester 2**

This module introduces students to the key digital marketing and eBusiness management issues facing decision-makers in a highly dynamic environment. The purpose is to provide students with skills, concepts, frameworks, and experiences that are useful for developing in the student the capacity to manage teams and agencies on digital marketing and e-business projects. Students are expected to attend workshops, webinars, complete independent reading and engage in applied learning activities on a regular basis.

**(5 ECTS)**

### Strategic Thinking and Marketing Strategies

**Semester 2**

The main objective of this module is to help develop the student's capacity to think strategically across a range of business and marketing contexts and to better understand the organisational and leadership challenges and complex issues involved in the process of managing strategically. Content of this module is delivered through lectures with an emphasis on digitally-focused case studies and in class presentations. Students are expected to attend lectures, engage in class discussions, and to complete various individual and group assignments. Each week a set of recommended readings accompanies each topic covered in the module.

**(5 ECTS)**

### AI and Responsible Leadership in Digital Marketing

**Semester 2**

This module focuses on responsible leadership in an AI context. It will equip learners with the knowledge, frameworks, and tools to effectively and responsibly integrate AI into their marketing practices while ensuring integrity and regulatory alignment.

**(5 ECTS)**
